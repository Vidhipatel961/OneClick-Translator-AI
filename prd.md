# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## OneClick Translator AI – Translate. Dub. Subtitle. Understand.

### 1. Product Vision & Objective
To create a single, intelligent, and easy-to-use platform that breaks down language barriers by providing multilingual translation, transcription, subtitles, voice dubbing, OCR, and document translation in one unified workflow.

### 2. Problem Statement
Language barriers make it difficult for individuals and businesses to understand and share information across different languages and media formats. Currently, users have to rely on multiple separate, fragmented tools to translate text, transcribe audio, subtitle videos, translate documents, and extract text from images or scanned PDFs. This process is time-consuming, expensive, and technically complex.

### 3. Proposed Solution
OneClick Translator AI is an AI-powered multilingual content transformation platform. Users can upload text, audio, video, PDFs, documents, or images, select their source and target languages, and let the platform automatically process the content using AI (Speech-to-Text, Machine Translation, OCR, Text-to-Speech, etc.).

### 4. Target Audience
- **Students & Educational Institutions**: Translating study materials and lectures.
- **Content Creators & YouTubers**: Generating subtitles and AI dubbing.
- **Businesses & Startups**: Translating business documents and marketing content.
- **Freelancers & Translation Professionals**: Speeding up workflows using translation memory.
- **Media & Marketing Agencies**: Localizing ads and campaigns.
- **Global Teams**: Translating meeting notes, audio, and documents.
- **Individuals**: Translating personal files and recordings.

### 5. Key Features (Functional Requirements)
1. **Multilingual Translation**: Translate text and extracted content into multiple languages.
2. **Audio Translation**: Convert speech into text and translate it.
3. **Video Localization**: Generate translated subtitles and AI voice dubbing.
4. **Document Translation**: Process PDFs and office documents while preserving formatting.
5. **Image & Scanned Document Translation**: OCR to extract and translate text.
6. **AI Assistant**: Help users understand, summarize, and interact with the content.
7. **Translation Memory**: Reuse previous translations for consistency.
8. **Project & History Management**: Organize translations and access history.

### 6. Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: React.js, TypeScript
- **Backend**: Python, FastAPI
- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **AI Models**: LLMs (Translation, Summarization), Speech-to-Text (STT), Text-to-Speech (TTS), Optical Character Recognition (OCR)

### 7. Expected Impact
Reduces the need to switch between specialized applications, making language accessibility faster, simpler, and more affordable globally.
